## Intent

chantalmasse.com — French-language marketing and booking site for Chantal Massé, therapist in Shefford (Nuxt, SSR on Vercel): a single landing page composed of sections, service pages, blog content, appointment booking (availability, create, cancel) against her calendar, trigger.dev jobs for reminders, review requests and a weekly brief, newsletter and contact routes, PostHog tracking. Edited by Jeremie; the client sends copy. Correctness bar: a booking is created once and confirmed by email, cancel links are single-use, no client PII in logs, pages fast on mobile.
